The assignment requires screenshots taken from the Simulator. They are intentionally not fabricated here.

After opening ClubConnect.xcodeproj in Xcode 27:
1. Run the welcome screen, enter a sample name, turn reminders ON/OFF, and choose Member or Officer. Take a screenshot.
2. Tap Join the Club and take a screenshot of the confirmation screen.
3. Run on two different iPhone screen sizes and capture both layouts.

If iPhone SE (3rd generation) or iPhone 15 Pro Max is not installed, use two other simulator devices with clearly different screen sizes, as the assignment lists those models only as examples.
