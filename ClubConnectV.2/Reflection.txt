In this iOS project, prepare(for:sender:) is used just before the segue finishes so the welcome screen can place the user’s name, reminder choice, and role into properties on the confirmation screen. The manual Show segue is identified by a storyboard identifier and is started by performSegue(withIdentifier:sender:), while the navigation controller automatically provides the Back button. In Android, a similar flow commonly uses an Intent with putExtra() to attach values and getStringExtra() or related methods to read them in the next Activity. Both approaches pass data during navigation, but iOS uses the destination view controller and segue lifecycle while Android commonly uses an Intent object between Activities.
